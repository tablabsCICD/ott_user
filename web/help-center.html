Help Center

Quick answers for accounts, installation, purchases and viewing.

How do I create or access my account?

Use the registration or login option shown by Filmytell and enter accurate contact details.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Check your internet connection and account details, then retry. Contact support if access cannot be restored.

How can I reset my password?

Select the available forgot-password option and complete the verification steps.

Which devices are supported?

Use a current supported web browser or an official Filmytell app. Availability may vary by platform.

What should I do if a video is buffering?

Check your network, restart playback and update your browser or app. Contact support with the title and device if it continues.

How do I change audio language?

When a title provides multiple audio tracks, choose one using the existing player language control.

How do I enable subtitles?

When subtitles are available, select them using the existing player subtitle control.

How do purchases or rentals work?

Sign in, select eligible content and follow the displayed access and payment steps. Access periods are shown with the title.

How does Continue Watching work?

Supported viewing progress appears in Continue Watching for your signed-in account.

How do I watch mini-series parts?

Select an available part and complete any required access step before playback.

How do I install the Filmytell app?

Use only an official Filmytell store listing supplied by Filmytell.

How can I contact support?

Email connect@filmytell.com. Never send passwords, verification codes or full card details.

Still need help?
