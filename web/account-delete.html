Delete Your Filmytell Account

Use this page to delete your Filmytell account or request deletion of the personal data connected with your account.

Email Deletion Request Open Filmytell

Delete From The App

  1. Open the Filmytell app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile, then Account Details.
  3. Choose Delete in the Account Deletion section.
  4. Confirm the deletion request. After completion, you will be logged out.

Request Deletion By Email

If you cannot access the app, email support@filmytell.com from your registered email address, or include your registered mobile number so we can verify the account.

We will process verified deletion requests and confirm once the account deletion is complete.

Data Deleted Or Retained

  • Deleted: profile details, account login details, preferences, and other personal account information associated with your Filmytell account.
  • Retained when required: purchase history, payment records, wallet transactions, support tickets, fraud-prevention records, tax records, and legal compliance records.
  • Retention period: legally required records may be retained for the period required by applicable law, business accounting, dispute resolution, security, or fraud-prevention obligations.

Deleting your account may remove access to purchased content, watch history, saved items, wallet access, and other account-based services.
