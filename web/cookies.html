Cookies and Browser Storage Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store through your browser.

Local storage and similar technologies

Flutter Web and related services may use local storage and similar browser technologies alongside cookies.

Essential session storage

Essential storage supports security, navigation, session continuity and core service operation.

Authentication storage

Authentication-related storage may help maintain account access and protect signed-in activity.

User preferences

Storage may remember language, display and other choices available in Filmytell.

Analytics

Analytics information may help us understand aggregate use and improve features. This page does not add any tracking technology.

Performance monitoring

Diagnostic signals may help identify errors, reliability problems and slow experiences.

Third-party services

Some providers may use their own technologies under their respective policies.

Managing cookies

You can remove or restrict cookies and storage in browser settings. Controls vary by browser.

Effects of disabling essential storage

Blocking required storage may prevent sign-in, preferences or other Filmytell Web features from working correctly.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as technology or service practices change.

Questions may be sent to connect@filmytell.com.
